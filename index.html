The Thesis

The next phase of AI is not more interaction — but less of it.

Instead of adding intelligence to interfaces, AI will move decision-making into the background. Users define preferences once. Systems learn from behavior. Then they operate continuously, surfacing only final choices when needed.

Spotify's Discover Weekly is the early example. We are extending this model across everyday decisions — reading, food, shopping, and lifestyle.
