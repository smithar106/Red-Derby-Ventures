Our Thesis

The next phase of AI isn't about doing more.
It's about needing less from you.

Smartphones were supposed to give us more time. Instead they gave us TikTok feeds, infinite scroll, and four hours of screen time we didn't ask for. We're watching the beginning of the reversal — people returning to dumb phones, app-free devices selling out, a growing exhaustion with managing everything just because you can access everything.

AI is going to accelerate this shift. Not by adding more intelligence to your screen, but by moving the work off it entirely. You spend ten minutes with a model. You share your preferences, your taste, how you make decisions. The model learns — then works in the background. Every few minutes it surfaces a choice: A or B. You confirm and disappear again. No feed. No notifications. No app to babysit.

The decision-making happens on the backend. The output shows up when it's ready. Not smarter phones. Not more powerful apps. Just your time back.

🎵
Spotify has already proven this model works. You rate a handful of songs, and Discover Weekly appears every Monday — no ongoing input required. The more you use it, the sharper it gets. You never manage it. It just works. Our four apps are built on the same principle: taste in, time back out. A reading identity that finds your next book. A style DNA that surfaces the right secondhand piece. A fridge scan that builds your week of meals. A drink profile that finds your NA match. Each one learns from a small moment of input and removes a recurring decision from your life permanently.
